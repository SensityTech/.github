This organization is dedicated to releasing internal tools, resources, and projects that we have decided to make publicly available, with the goal of helping communities grow, improve, and build better experiences.

Founded in 2018, Sensity has built over eight years of experience in the gaming industry. Throughout the years, we have developed strong expertise in game design, feature development, technical infrastructure, and the maintenance of large-scale gaming environments.

Sensity has continuously worked to stand out through its concepts, systems, and technical solutions, with a particular focus on Rockstar Games titles and the various projects and communities we have built around them.

We hope that the tools and resources we share here will make your work easier and contribute to the development of your own projects.

If you encounter any issues or have questions regarding our releases, our team remains available to help.
